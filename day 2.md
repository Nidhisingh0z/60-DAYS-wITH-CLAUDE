Section 1: What Is Prompt Engineering?
Think of AI like a very smart assistant who does exactly what you ask — but only what you ask. If your instructions are vague, you get vague results. If your instructions are clear and specific, you get something genuinely useful.
Prompt Engineering is simply the skill of writing better instructions for AI tools like Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini. It's not coding. It's not technical. It's about learning how to talk to AI in a way that gets you the best possible output.
Why does it matter? Most people use AI like a basic search engine — short, unclear phrases. But AI can do so much more when you guide it properly. Better prompts = better results, saved time, and less frustration.

Section 3: Three Practical Benefits of Better Prompts

Save Time — You get usable output on the first try instead of going back and forth five times to fix vague results.
Get Higher Quality — Specific prompts unlock AI's full potential. You move from "good enough" to "actually impressive."
Do More With Less — You don't need to be a tech expert. One well-crafted prompt can replace hours of manual work in writing, research, brainstorming, and more.
