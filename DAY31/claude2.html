🛰️ Welcome, Head of Operations

A 3-minute live simulation of running a global supply chain.

Alerts will stream in from ports, factories, warehouses, and transport routes. Each one threatens your service level, customer satisfaction, and revenue. You must decide fast — the right call protects the business, the wrong call (or no call at all) makes things worse.

How it works

  • Alerts appear with a priority (Medium / High / Critical) and a countdown timer.
  • Choose an action before time runs out — each alert offers different response options.
  • As the clock ticks down, alerts arrive faster and get more severe.
  • Delaying a decision doesn't remove the problem — it just postpones the consequences.

Your KPIs

  • Service Level / Satisfaction / Inventory / Transport Efficiency — higher is better.
  • Operating Cost Index — lower is better (100 = baseline).
  • Revenue Protected — cumulative dollar impact of your decisions.
  • Score — your overall performance rating for the shift.

❓ Instructions

Quick reference for running the control tower.

Alert Priorities

  • Medium — manageable, longer countdown, lower stakes.
  • High — significant exposure, moderate countdown.
  • Critical — severe exposure, short countdown, pulses red.

Actions

  • Expedite Shipment / Approve Air Freight — fast but costly fixes.
  • Use Backup Supplier / Increase Production / Transfer Inventory / Reroute Trucks — targeted operational fixes; effective only when matched to the right problem.
  • Ignore — always the worst option; the problem escalates immediately.
  • Delay Decision — buys a few seconds, but the consequences catch up to you later.

Scoring

Every action shifts your KPIs and Score. Best-fit actions earn the most points and protect the most revenue. Mismatched actions, ignoring alerts, and letting alerts expire all hurt your KPIs and Score — ignoring and expiration hurt the most.
