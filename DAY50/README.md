Day 50/100 — Build: Defend Your Experience

Most interview prep tools help you write a better resume.

This one assumes your resume is already fine — and attacks it anyway.

Defend Your Experience is an AI-powered adaptive interview simulator. You paste in your resume, and it doesn't review it. It cross-examines it.

Here's what happens:

→ Every line on your resume becomes an "exhibit" — a claim you now have to defend → An AI interviewer picks your weakest, vaguest, or most unproven claim and comes after it first → Every answer you give shapes the next question — follow-ups get sharper and more specific the longer you go → A live confidence score tracks, claim by claim, how well-defended your story actually is → At the end, you get a Defense Report: what's rock solid, what's still shaky, and exactly how to fix it before a real interviewer finds the gap first

No generic "tell me about yourself" templates. No canned questions. The entire interview is generated from your own experience — so the pressure feels real, because it's built entirely out of things you actually said about yourself.

The goal isn't a shinier resume. It's the confidence that comes from knowing every claim you make about yourself can survive a real grilling.

Built as a single self-contained app, running entirely on Claude — no backend, no server, just the model doing the interviewing in real time.

50 days in. Still building in public, one uncomfortable feature at a time.
