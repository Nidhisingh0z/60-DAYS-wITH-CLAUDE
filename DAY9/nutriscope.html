Recommendations

Educational Use Only

NutriScope provides general nutritional estimates for educational and self-tracking purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent any disease or condition. Nutrient values are approximate averages and can vary by brand, preparation method, and portion size. Calorie and micronutrient targets are generic population estimates (Mifflin-St Jeor equation and standard RDA/RDI references) and do not account for individual medical conditions, pregnancy, lactation, or clinical deficiencies. "Risk" flags in this app are simple rule-based pattern indicators derived from your logged intake — they are not a diagnosis. Always consult a registered dietitian, nutritionist, or physician before making significant dietary changes, especially if you have an existing health condition.

Reference Sources

  • Indian Council of Medical Research (ICMR) – National Institute of Nutrition (NIN): Recommended Dietary Allowances and Estimated Average Requirements for Indians.
  • Mifflin-St Jeor Equation (1990) for Basal Metabolic Rate estimation.
  • Institute of Medicine (IOM) / NIH Office of Dietary Supplements: Dietary Reference Intakes (DRIs) for vitamins and minerals.
  • USDA FoodData Central: general food composition reference ranges used to approximate per-100g nutrient values.
  • World Health Organization (WHO): activity-level energy expenditure multipliers.

How Targets Are Calculated

BMR — Mifflin-St Jeor: Men = 10×kg + 6.25×cm − 5×age + 5. Women = 10×kg + 6.25×cm − 5×age − 161.

TDEE — BMR × activity multiplier (1.2 – 1.9).

Macros — Protein scaled to body weight & activity, ~50% remaining calories to carbs, ~27% to fat.

Micronutrients — fixed RDA bands by age/gender (e.g. iron 8mg men / 18mg women, calcium 1000mg, B12 2.4mcg).
