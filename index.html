      ⚠️ Biggest anomaly

      Mumbai sensor fault, Jul 2 2026: One citizen-network station briefly reported AQI 999 / PM2.5 837µg/m³ — physically implausible and far outside every neighbouring station's range (30–107). Flagged as a sensor malfunction and excluded; Mumbai's verified reading of AQI 64 (aqi.in city dashboard) is used throughout.

      😮 Most surprising observation

      Ghaziabad out-pollutes Delhi. A satellite city of ~1.7M people (AQI 179) is currently dirtier than the capital megacity of ~33M it borders (AQI 162) — proximity to industrial clusters and NCR traffic corridors can matter more than sheer city size.

      📉 Trend read

      Coastal / peninsular cities (Bengaluru, Chennai, Mumbai, Hyderabad) cluster in Good–Satisfactory AQI, while the Indo-Gangetic belt (Delhi NCR, Jaipur, Lucknow, Ghaziabad) clusters in Moderate–Poor — consistent with the well-documented winter/pre-monsoon pollution trapping in the Himalaya-adjacent plains.
