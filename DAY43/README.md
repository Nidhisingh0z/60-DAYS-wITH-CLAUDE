Day 43 – AI Workflow Architect 🌟
Today’s challenge: Designing Complete AI-Powered Workflows 🚀
💡 The app generates structured workflows with:
Objectives, tasks, and AI tool recommendations
Interactive diagrams, decision trees, and progress tracking
Best practices, common mistakes, and efficiency tips
Workflow summary, AI stack, resources, and future automation opportunities
✨ Features:
Responsive design with dark mode
Smooth animations & intuitive UI/UX
Local storage + printable workflow guides
This project combines my interests in AI, automation, and frontend development—turning abstract processes into actionable, interactive workflows.
