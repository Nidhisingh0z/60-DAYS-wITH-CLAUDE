Workflow summary

This plan takes a portfolio site from a blank page to a deployed, tested build in six stages: discovery defines who the site is for and what it must contain; content and copy get drafted before a single div is written; design locks the visual system; two build stages turn that system into semantic HTML/CSS and then layer on JavaScript interactivity; and the final stage covers QA, performance, SEO and deployment. AI tools are woven into every stage — for research and content in the early stages, code generation and debugging in the build stages, and auditing in the final stage — but every AI output is treated as a first draft for a human to review, not a finished deliverable.
