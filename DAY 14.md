5 Smart Interview Questions

"Could you walk me through the specific split of responsibilities across DevOps, QA, and support — is this one role or is there a team I'd be working alongside?"
"What does 'available nights/weekends as needed' look like in a typical month, and is on-call time compensated?"
"Can you share the current headcount and most recent funding round details? I saw some inconsistency between your site and LinkedIn."
"What's the salary range for this role, and is it fixed or negotiable based on experience?"
"Can I speak with someone currently in this role or a similar one about day-to-day workload and turnover on the team?"
