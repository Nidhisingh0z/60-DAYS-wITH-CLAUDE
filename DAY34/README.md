🔍 Day 34: Become a Marketing Detective
Marketing isn’t just about campaigns—it’s about solving mysteries. Every click, every bounce, every conversion tells a story. Today, I put on my detective hat to investigate:
Why do some ads perform better than others?
What clues do customer behaviors leave behind?
How can data reveal the hidden motives behind buying decisions?
By treating marketing like a mystery, I uncover insights that aren’t obvious at first glance. The thrill isn’t just in finding answers—it’s in asking the right questions.
🕵️‍♀️ Marketing detectives don’t guess. They observe, analyze, and connect the dots.
