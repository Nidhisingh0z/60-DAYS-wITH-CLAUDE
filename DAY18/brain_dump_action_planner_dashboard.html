Summary

The leadership team reviewed Q2 performance, where revenue grew 12% against an 18% target, driven largely by enterprise deals slipping into next quarter (9 of 14 expected contracts closed). Marketing reported a 34% increase in website traffic but weaker conversion rates. Customer retention remained strong at 94%, though support ticket volume increased, mainly linked to reporting performance and dashboard loading times. Engineering capacity, the August analytics dashboard timeline, hiring plans, and the annual customer conference budget were also discussed, with several decisions deferred pending further information.
