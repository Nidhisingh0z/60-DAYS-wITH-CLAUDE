 Day 44 of my learning journey:
I built an AI-Powered LinkedIn Profile Optimizer — a tool designed to turn any LinkedIn profile into a recruiter magnet.

🔍 What it does:

Roasts your current profile with honest recruiter-style feedback

Rebuilds headline, about section, experience, and skills with proven templates

Provides a 7-day activation plan to boost visibility and engagement

Generates a shareable “Before vs After” scorecard

💡 Why it matters:
Most profiles just sit there. This project helps professionals transform their LinkedIn into something that actually works for them — attracting recruiters, clients, and collaborators.
