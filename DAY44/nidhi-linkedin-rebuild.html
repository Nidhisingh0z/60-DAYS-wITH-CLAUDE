Review your numbers

Check Profile Views and connection acceptance rate in your Analytics tab.

What the numbers mean:
• Profile views jumped → your headline and posts are getting you found. Keep posting weekly.
• Views flat but connections accepted → your profile converts once seen, but visibility is still the bottleneck — comment more on others' posts.
• Views up but few connections accepted → your headline/About may be strong but your ask (connection note) isn't landing — tighten the template.
• Everything flat → give it one more week of consistent posting before changing anything; LinkedIn's algorithm rewards consistency over single posts.
