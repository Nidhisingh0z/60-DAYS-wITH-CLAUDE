Day 45/60: Build an AI Decision Strategist

Think Better. Decide Smarter.

Today's build: a prompt that turns Claude into an impartial Decision Strategist — one that doesn't just tell you what you want to hear.

How it works:
→ It interviews you with 4 sharp questions (one at a time, no rambling)
→ It builds a full Decision Report: case for each option, your hidden biases, a scored decision matrix, a premortem, and a real action plan
→ It ends with a decisive verdict — not "it depends," an actual answer

I tested it live on a real decision: go to a job interview tomorrow while sick and out of town, or skip it.

The output didn't just validate my gut. It caught an assumption I hadn't even noticed I was making — that skipping meant the door was closed for good, when I hadn't even asked if it could be rescheduled.

That's the difference between an AI that agrees with you and one that actually helps you think.

Full interactive report (with animated scoring, premortem, and a 7-point decision matrix) built entirely from a single prompt.

Would you trust an AI to help you make a hard call? Or is that a job only a human should do?
