Day 36: Explore Your Thinking Patterns

Today’s challenge was about stepping back and noticing how we think.
Not the answers we give, but the processes we use when facing decisions and uncertainty.

✨ Key reflections:

Do I rely more on intuition or analysis?

How do I handle incomplete information?

What patterns emerge when I’m under pressure?

Where do biases sneak in unnoticed?

By mapping these thinking patterns, I discovered the hidden “algorithms” behind my choices. Once you see them, you can refine them—just like debugging code.

🔍 The real growth isn’t in avoiding uncertainty, but in learning how your mind navigates it.
